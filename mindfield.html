MINDFIELD measures your stress level and identifies exactly which parts of your life are driving it. 53 questions. Honest results.

How are you really doing?

MINDFIELD is a stress assessment for students. It measures stress across 18 areas of your life — from sleep and health to school, home, and social pressure — and tells you where the load is heaviest.

Your answers are run through a machine learning model trained on data from real-world stress research, which classifies your overall stress level and ranks the factors contributing to it.

Answer honestly. There are no wrong answers, and your data never leaves this page.
